Honest note

Why isn’t it on the Play Store yet?

One simple reason, told straight: Google’s one-time developer fee is $25, and I don’t have it today.

The reason: $25

Publishing on the Play Store costs a one-time $25 developer registration. NO Wallet is a one-person project with no funding, no ads and no investors, so that fee isn’t paid yet.

The promise

The day I can pay it, NO Wallet goes live on the Play Store too, and every Download button on this site will switch to the store listing. Until then, the buttons here get you the app directly.

No ads. No investors. No shortcuts. Just an app that stays yours.
