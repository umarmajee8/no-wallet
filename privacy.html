Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026 · E Wallet

1. The short version

E Wallet does not collect, store or sell any of your data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server anywhere in the loop. Everything you add lives on your phone and nowhere else.

2. Your cards and pictures

  • Cards (ID, licence, student card, bank cards) and their pictures are stored only on your device.
  • They never leave your phone unless you share them yourself, for example over WhatsApp or by saving an image.
  • E Wallet works fully offline; no internet connection is required to open or use your cards.

3. Backups

A backup file holds your cards and their pictures, encrypted with a password only you know. You choose where that file goes: Drive, Files or WhatsApp. We cannot read it, open it or recover it for anyone, including you. Keep the password safe.

Honest note: the 4-digit code locks the app, not the stored data. It is the backup password that encrypts the file.

4. Permissions

The app may ask for permission to pick images from your gallery (or use the camera) so you can add cards. Those images are processed on the device and are never uploaded.

5. Children

E Wallet collects nothing from anyone, of any age. There is simply no data to protect, because none ever leaves your phone.
